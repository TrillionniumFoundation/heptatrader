# Train V2 Phase 2 - Original source, model parity and economic review

2026-10-10. Isolated pocket4 research branch stacked on Train V2 Phase 1. Not installed or merged into the trading runtime. No account, order, model, source guard or existing forward trial changes.

## Feature compatibility is NOT tensor or policy equivalence

The original Numeric price learner uses a fixed 16-dimensional causal feature profile and 300/900-second future-MID price targets. Laya uses the original seven-bin price question; the pretrained ACT/ESCALATE auxiliary head is not the broker BUY/SELL decision. Financial Value EUR25k and USD100k use distinct original behavior-policy identities and closed, fee-complete realized net USD reward populations.

Phase 2 uses the unchanged original Numeric feature and Laya question implementations to reconstruct both representations from the same locally verified causal original quote sequence. It does not reinterpret V2's generic four-value feature vector as Numeric16, nor a seven-bin probability as an economic value or action permission. Cross-family model selection is denied.

This establishes exact original feature construction, not model forward-result numerical parity or economic performance.

## Original local PAPER chain handoff

An independent one-event CLI validates the old locally hash-chained PAPER quote source, recompiles the claimed dataset feature, checks recorded source receipt identity and timing, and refuses an event presented more than three seconds after its claimed event timestamp. A pre-decision source event must still carry a PENDING label, not a fabricated profitable outcome; a future outcome requires the original matured quote and strict horizon/endpoint evidence. Locally untrusted corrections cannot pass this path.

The local chain is not signed broker market data, not an independently enrolled producer identity and not a canonical Agentd LearningLedger. A privileged local user could create another internally consistent source file. Consequently this stage is a strictly read-only research admission interface, not a deployed producer hook or automatically trained model.

Original Trial3, Laya, Neuron256 and other prospective plans retain their prior missed/late cases and denominators; no retroactive repair or re-registration is allowed.

## Original PAPER cost and economic distinction

The diagnostic cost profile requires an exact PAPER accounting-period cost receipt derived from original quote chains and deduplicated broker commission callbacks, not revised IB historical bar spreads. Fee completeness and positive source coverage are necessary. Original exchange execution time and actual slippage are not attested.

A fixed gross price forecast is compared against no trade under p90 original quote spread, p90 broker fee and assumed 0.5/1 basis points per side; the stress case uses p99 spread and assumed 2 bps slippage per side. Small modeled edges receive RESEARCH_ABSTAIN_COST_GATE. Even when every stress scenario is positive, the result is UNQUALIFIED_POSITIVE_MODELED_EDGE with no broker-order, actor, model-selection or promotion authority. This is neither actual strategy PnL nor complete account return.

## Required next acceptance

Future live producer registration must occur before a genuinely new event and must have independent host owner trust, exact original REQUEST/PREPARED deadlines and a bounded GPU lease. Actual value and broker PnL joins require full original commission and correction lineage, realistic execution costs, fresh quotes and complete account statement evidence. Economic admission requires independent future samples and a net advantage over no-trade under predeclared risk limits. Canonical Agentd owner selection, rollback, revocation and exact CI remain separately uncompleted.
